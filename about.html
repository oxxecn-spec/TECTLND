About MBORMC — Global Custom Mosquito Screens & Pet-Proof Door Manufacturer

From our Foshan factory to your doorstep — trusted custom protection for 40+ countries worldwide

Manufacturing Excellence Since 2010

MBORMC is a leading global manufacturer of high-quality custom mosquito screens, invisible dust-proof screens, pet-proof screen doors, entry folding screen doors, retractable screens, and window blinds. Our factory is located in Chancheng District, Foshan, China — a region renowned for its advanced manufacturing capabilities and rich industrial heritage.

With over 15 years of experience, we have grown into a trusted supplier for families, businesses, and OEM partners across 40+ countries. We are proud to hold BSCI and ISO9001 certifications, ensuring our production processes meet the highest international standards for quality, safety, and social responsibility.

Our mission is simple: to provide custom protection for every home — combining precision engineering, durable materials, and global logistics to deliver custom mosquito screens and pet-proof folding doors to your doorstep, wherever you are.
