Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about custom mosquito screens, pet-proof doors, ordering, shipping, and more

Yes! We accept private custom orders with no minimum quantity. Simply send us your measurements and we'll provide a quote within 24 hours. Whether you need one screen or one hundred, every order is handled with the same care and precision.
For large-scale projects, MOQ is flexible. We can start with 10–50 pieces for trial orders, then scale to thousands. Contact us to discuss your project requirements — we'll work with you to find the best solution.
Simply send us your window or door measurements (width and height), choose your preferred mesh type and frame color, and we'll provide a detailed quote. Once confirmed, we'll start production and ship directly to your address.
We ship to 40+ countries worldwide including the USA, UK, Australia, Canada, Germany, France, Spain, Italy, Singapore, Malaysia, Japan, and many more. If your country isn't listed, contact us — we can likely ship to you too.
We offer multiple shipping options to suit your needs:
  • Sea Freight — Most economical for bulk orders (20FT/40FT containers), 15–35 days
  • Air Freight — Fast and reliable for urgent orders, 5–10 days
  • Rail — Cost-effective for Europe and Central Asia, 18–25 days
  • Express (DHL/FedEx) — Door-to-door for samples and small orders, 3–7 days
Shipping costs depend on your location, order size, and chosen shipping method. We provide a detailed shipping quote with every order. For bulk orders, we can help you find the most cost-effective shipping solution.
We offer two main mesh types:
  • PET Electrostatic Dust‑Proof Mesh — Standard option. Captures dust, pollen, and PM2.5 particles. Suitable for most homes.
  • 304 Stainless Steel Pet‑Proof Mesh — Scratch and bite resistant. Ideal for households with dogs, cats, or other pets.
