Perfect Self-Adhesive Screen for Every Need

From rental apartments to temporary protection – affordable, removable, effective

🏠 Rental Properties & Temporary Housing

For renters, the FD02 is the ultimate removable insect screen. Unlike permanent frames, the self‑adhesive Velcro system leaves no holes, no marks, and no damage to window frames or walls. When you move out, simply peel it off and take it with you – or leave it for the next tenant. Landlords can supply FD02 screens to new tenants quickly without hiring contractors.

The 304 stainless steel mesh provides long‑lasting protection against mosquitoes, flies, and other insects. The high‑bond tape works on aluminium, uPVC, wood, and painted surfaces. For rental properties, the FD02 is a cost‑effective way to keep tenants satisfied and reduce insect‑related complaints.

🚐 Caravans, Campers & Temporary Enclosures

For travellers and outdoor enthusiasts, the FD02 offers a portable mosquito screen solution. The flexible mesh and tape system can be trimmed to fit caravan windows, campervan doors, or pop‑up tents. When not in use, roll it up and store it – no bulky frames to carry. The 304 stainless steel mesh is resilient and won't tear during travel.

Many campers use the FD02 to screen their outdoor awnings or gazebo sides. The self‑adhesive tape attaches to poles and fabric, creating a temporary insect barrier that's easy to remove when packing up.

🏡 Windows & Doors – DIY Installation

For homeowners who want a quick, affordable solution, the FD02 is the perfect DIY window screen. No measuring skills required – simply cut the mesh to size with regular scissors, peel off the tape backing, and press it onto your frame. The hook‑and‑loop system allows you to remove the screen for cleaning or during winter months when insect protection is not needed.

The washable design means you can rinse dust and pollen off the mesh, keeping it clear and effective. Unlike traditional fixed screens that accumulate dirt, the FD02 can be maintained with minimal effort. For sliding doors and French doors, the FD02 can be customised to cover the entire opening, with the mesh folding or rolling when not in use.

🏨 Hotels, Hostels & Short‑Stay Accommodation

Hospitality businesses value the FD02 for its quick installation and removability. For seasonal properties, screens can be installed before summer and removed in winter. The self‑adhesive system means no structural changes – perfect for heritage buildings with strict conservation rules.

Hostels and budget accommodations can order FD02 screens in bulk at wholesale prices, with custom sizing to fit different window types. The 304 stainless steel mesh is durable enough for high‑turnover environments, and the screens can be washed between guest stays.
