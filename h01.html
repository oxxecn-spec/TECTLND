Perfect Invisible Screen for Every Setting

From private homes to hotels – clean, dust‑free, and pet‑safe

🏡 Private Villas & Luxury Homes

For villa owners, the H01 is the ultimate invisible screen – when rolled up, it disappears completely, preserving your unobstructed views. The optional PET electrostatic dust‑proof mesh actively captures dust, pollen, and PM2.5, keeping indoor air cleaner. And because it's removable and washable, you can rinse it under a tap and reinstall it in minutes.

For pet owners, the 304 stainless steel pet‑proof mesh resists scratching and biting – even from large dogs. The tool‑free adhesive installation means you can fit the screens yourself without drilling or hiring contractors.

🏨 Hotels, Resorts & Hospitality

Hotels and resorts across 50+ countries trust MBORMC as their custom screen supplier. The H01 is perfect for guest rooms, suites, restaurants, and conference areas. Guests enjoy fresh air without dust, pollen, or insects entering.

For hotel chains, we offer OEM branding: laser engrave your logo on the cassette, print your brand on packaging, or custom colour matching. Factory direct pricing and low MOQ (from 50 pieces) make trial orders easy.

🏢 Apartments & Condominiums

For apartment dwellers, the H01 is the perfect window screen and door screen solution. Renters love the adhesive installation – no damage to walls or frames, fully removable. Landlords can supply H01 screens to all units quickly because the adhesive works on aluminium, PVC, wood, and metal frames.

For large condominium projects (200+ units), we can produce and ship within 20 days with wooden crate protection.

🌳 Dust & Pollen Protection

Unlike ordinary mosquito screens, the H01's PET electrostatic mesh captures microscopic particles – dust, pollen, PM2.5, and even pet dander – by attracting them to the fibre surface. This means less dust on your window sills, less cleaning, and healthier indoor air.

When the mesh gets dirty, simply remove and wash it – no special tools required. This makes the H01 a low‑maintenance, long‑lasting solution.

🌍 Global Compatibility

The H01 works with all common window types: UK sash windows, US sliding windows, Australian casement windows, European tilt‑and‑turn, and inward/outward opening windows. For patio doors or sliding doors, we offer reinforced frames and wider cassettes.

Our team provides free technical support via WhatsApp and installation videos in multiple languages. Whether you need a single screen or 5,000 pieces, MBORMC delivers consistent quality.
