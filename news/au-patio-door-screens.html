The Australian Door Screen Challenge

Australian homes feature a variety of door styles, each presenting unique challenges for insect screens:

  • French doors — Popular in period homes (Queenslanders, Federation, Victorian) and modern extensions. Two doors open outward or inward. Traditional screens are difficult to install without blocking both doors.
  • Patio doors (sliding) — Common in modern homes and extensions. The sliding mechanism requires screens that don't interfere with operation.
  • Bi-fold doors — Increasingly popular for alfresco areas. Multiple panels fold to one side, making traditional screens impossible.
  • Stacker doors — Multi-panel sliding doors common in Queensland and contemporary designs.

Traditional Australian door screens fail in one or more of these key areas:

  • Floor tracks — Collect dirt, create tripping hazards, and require regular cleaning
  • Bulky frames — Obstruct views and compromise the aesthetic of the doors
  • Fixed screens — Block access and make it impossible to fully open doors
  • No insect protection — Many Australian homes simply go without screens, leaving doors open to mosquitoes

Bi-Fold Doors — The Ultimate Australian Test

Bi-fold doors are increasingly popular in Australian homes, particularly for alfresco entertaining areas . They present the ultimate test for insect screens:

  • Multiple panels — Each panel folds to one side
  • No central frame — When fully open, there's no vertical structure for a screen to mount to
  • Large openings — Standard bi-fold doors can be 3-6 meters wide

Traditional solutions fail on bi-fold doors because they:

  • Require a floor track — Creates a tripping hazard across the full width
  • Block the view — Bulky frames obstruct the glass
  • Prevent full opening — Fixed screens block the fold

The H01's top-hung zipper track design works with bi-fold doors because:

  • No floor track — Safe and clean
  • Narrow cassette — Sits discreetly at the top of the door frame
  • Zipper guides — Mount on the side frames, not the folding panels
  • Invisible when retracted — Preserves the view
  • Compatible with outdoor entertaining — When the doors are fully open, the screen is retracted and out of the way
