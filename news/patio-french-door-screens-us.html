The Challenge of Large Openings – Why Traditional Screens Don't Work

French doors and patio doors are typically much wider than standard windows – often 1.5 to 3 meters (5 to 10 feet) or more. Traditional solutions for these large openings are problematic:

  • Fixed screens – They're bulky, block the view, and require a floor track that collects dirt and creates a tripping hazard.
  • Sliding screen doors – They're heavy, hard to operate, and require floor tracks that trap debris.
  • Retractable screens with floor tracks – Many retractable screens use bottom tracks, which collect dust, are hard to clean, and can be damaged by foot traffic.
  • No screen at all – Most common solution, but it leaves your home vulnerable to mosquitoes and other insects.

The H01 solves these problems with its innovative top-hung design with zipper guide rails. There's no floor track – just side guide rails and a top cassette. This means:

  • No tripping hazard – no rail on the floor to trip over
  • No debris accumulation – no floor track to trap dirt
  • Easy cleaning – floors can be swept and mopped freely
  • Universal compatibility – works with inward and outward-opening doors

🔧 Zipper Track vs Floor Track: Unlike traditional retractable screens that use a floor track (which collects dirt, traps debris, and creates a tripping hazard), the H01 uses top-hung zipper guide rails. These rails guide the screen smoothly into place without any bottom track – keeping your floor clear and safe.

Bi-Parting Doors – A Special Challenge Solved

Many French doors are bi-parting – both doors open outward or inward. This creates a special challenge for screens, as there's no central mullion to attach a screen to. The H01 can be installed on bi-parting doors using a specialized center guide that sits between the two doors when closed, and recedes when the doors are opened – allowing full operation without interference.

Global Mosquito Threat – Why Screens for Doors Are Essential

Mosquitoes don't just come through windows – they fly right through open doors. In fact, doors are often the easiest entry point for mosquitoes, especially in homes with French doors that are frequently opened.

The global mosquito threat is staggering. According to the World Health Organization:

  • Over 700,000 people die from mosquito-borne diseases annually.
  • Malaria alone accounts for over 600,000 deaths per year, mostly in sub-Saharan Africa.
  • Dengue cases have increased 30-fold over the last 50 years, with over 5 million cases reported globally in 2023.
  • West Nile virus is now endemic in all 48 contiguous US states.
  • Zika remains a threat, with local transmission in Florida, Texas, and Hawaii.

In the US, the threat is growing. The CDC reports that mosquito-borne disease cases have tripled between 2004 and 2016, and climate change is expanding mosquito habitats northward. A study of 239 US cities found that 95% saw an increase in "mosquito disease days" – days warm enough for mosquito-borne disease transmission.

🌍 2025 Global Surveillance Data: In the first half of 2025, the world recorded over 2 million suspected dengue cases with over 1,000 deaths, over 200,000 chikungunya cases, and over 8,500 Zika cases across the Americas and Africa. The threat is real and growing.

DIY Installation – No Drilling, No Tools Required

Installing the H01 on your French doors or patio doors is simple:

  1. Clean the door frame surface where the adhesive tape will be applied.
  2. Peel the protective backing from the double-sided tape.
  3. Mount the cassette at the top of your door frame – inside or outside, depending on your preference.
  4. Install the zipper guide rails on each side of the door opening.
  5. Use – pull the screen down when needed, roll it up when not.

That's it. No drilling, no screws, no professional tools required. The entire installation takes minutes, not hours. And because it's adhesive-based, it's completely removable – no damage to your door frames.
