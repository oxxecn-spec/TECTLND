Retractable Pet-Proof Screen Door

MBORMC® H05 is a retractable screen door designed for large doorways, ideal for sliding patio doors, villa entrances, entry doors, living room French doors, offices, private spaces, studies, and sunrooms. Features high-strength PET fiber mesh for effective mosquito and insect protection, with pet-proof design that resists scratching and pushing. The chain-link retractable mechanism is available in single or double opening — space-saving and convenient. Track-mount installation requires drilling for maximum stability and durability.
